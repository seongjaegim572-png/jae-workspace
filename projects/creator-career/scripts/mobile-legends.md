# New account: Mobile Legends — 30 scripts (draft)

**Handle ideas (Jae creates):** `@koreanplaysml`, `@jae.mlbb`, `@kbro.mlbb`
**ONE person:** A Filipino or Indonesian MLBB player, 15 to 25, who loves K-culture.
**Reason to follow:** A Korean guy learning MLBB from you, honest about how bad (and good) he is.
**Filming:** Phone screen recording + 1 to 2 sec face reactions. No face needed for most.
**Placeholders:** [hero], [rank], [item] = Jae fills with real game info. Claude does not know the current meta.
**Safety:** blur player names and toxic chat.

---

## A. The Korean angle (share engine)

**1. Koreans don't play Mobile Legends**
- Hook: "Koreans don't play Mobile Legends. So I tried it." `[Mino: Is it possible to X?]`
- Beats: first match clips, confused face → "Filipino friends, which hero should I learn?"

**2. Korean guy vs Filipino trash talk**
- Text hook: `a Korean guy reads Filipino MLBB chat for the first time`
- Beats: blurred chat → Jae's face trying to translate

**3. Learning Taglish from MLBB chat**
- Hook: "I learned 3 Filipino words from MLBB chat. Are they right?"
- Comment trigger: correct me

**4. Korean PC bang vs my phone**
- Beats: PC bang B-roll (civilian clothes, normal PC bang) → phone game. "Koreans play this. I play this."

**5. Playing the hero you told me to**
- Text hook: `you said [hero]. I listened.`
- Uses top comment from #1

**6. My mom watches me play MLBB**
- Skit: Jae as Korean mom, confused by the map

**7. What Koreans think MLBB is**
- Hook: "I asked 3 Korean friends what Mobile Legends is." (only with friends' OK, or text answers on screen)

**8. Korean reacts to M-series hype**
- Hook: "Koreans have LCK. You have MPL. Here's what shocked me."
- Talking head, simple, no match footage unless allowed
