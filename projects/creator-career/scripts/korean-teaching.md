# New account: teaching Korean — 30 scripts (draft)

**Handle ideas (Jae creates):** `@koreanwithjae`, `@jae.speaks.korean`, `@realkorean.jae`
**ONE person:** A K-drama or K-pop fan, 16 to 30, who knows a few words but feels lost when real Koreans talk.
**Reason to follow:** A Korean guy teaches the Korean your textbook skips, with skits.
**On screen for every word:** Hangul (big) / romanization / meaning, 3 lines.
**Length:** 12 to 20 sec. One word or one idea per video.

---

## A. Words nobody explains (share engine)

**1. 3 words your Korean friend never explains**
- Hook: "Your Korean friend says these every day and never explains them."
- 헐 (heol) = "no way" / 대박 (daebak) = "insane" / 아 진짜? (a jinjja?) = "wait, really?"
- Ask: "Which one did you hear in a K-drama?" · Share to: your K-drama friend

**2. ㅋㅋㅋ explained**
- Text hook: `why your Korean friend texts ㅋㅋㅋ`
- Phone screen recording: ㅋ = dry / ㅋㅋ = polite laugh / ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ = actually funny

**3. Texting slang in 15 seconds**
- ㅇㅇ (yes) / ㄴㄴ (no no) / ㄱㄱ (let's go) / ㅇㅋ (ok)

**4. What 미쳤어 really means**
- Hook: "Koreans say 'you're crazy' three different ways. Only one is an insult."
- 미쳤어 (michyeosseo) acted 3 ways: angry / amazed / flirty

**5. 오빠 vs 형**
- Hook: "Why Korean guys get weird when you call them oppa."
- 오빠 (oppa) = girl to older guy / 형 (hyeong) = guy to older guy / one skit

**6. 밥 먹었어? isn't about food**
- Hook: "When a Korean asks if you ate, they're not asking about food."
- 밥 먹었어? (bap meogeosseo?) = "how are you, I care"

**7. 아이고**
- Hook: "The one word every Korean grandma uses for everything."
- 아이고 (aigo): tired / shocked / sad / proud, 4 quick cuts

**8. 화이팅**
- 화이팅 (hwaiting) = "you got this." Show the fist pump.

**9. 눈치**
- Hook: "Koreans have a word for reading the room. English doesn't."
- 눈치 (nunchi), skit: friend keeps talking while everyone else is leaving

**10. 정 (jeong)**
- Hook: "This Korean word has no English translation."
- 정 (jeong) = the bond that grows over time. Calm music (Clair de Lune).

## B. Textbook vs real (save engine)

**11. Ordering coffee**
- Textbook: 아이스 아메리카노 한 잔 주세요 / Real: 아아 하나요 (a-a hana-yo)
- Skit: Jae plays customer and barista

**12. Saying "I'm hungry" 3 ways**
- 배고파 (baegopa) / 배고파 죽겠어 (baegopa jukgesseo, "I'm dying") / 출출하다 (chulchulhada, "a bit peckish")

**13. Saying "I love you" without saying it**
- 보고 싶어 (bogo sipeo, "I miss you") / 잘 자 (jal ja, "sleep well") / 밥은? (babeun?, "did you eat?")

**14. How to say no politely in Korean**
- 괜찮아요 (gwaenchanayo) = "I'm good" (often means no)

**15. 3 ways to say thank you**
- 고마워 (casual) / 고마워요 (polite) / 감사합니다 (formal), with who to use each with

**16. 반말 vs 존댓말 in 15 sec**
- Hook: "Say this to the wrong person in Korea and it gets awkward."
- Same sentence, two levels, two reactions

**17. Korean age, explained**
- Hook: "I used to be 2 years older in Korea. Here's why."
- Note: since 2023 Korea officially uses international age, so say "used to"

**18. Calling a waiter**
- 저기요 (jeogiyo) = "excuse me." Show the slight hand raise.

**19. Numbers: two systems**
- Hook: "Korean has two number systems, and you need both to buy coffee."
- 하나 둘 셋 (count items) vs 일 이 삼 (prices), quick example

**20. Hangul in 20 sec: 5 vowels**
- ㅏ ㅓ ㅗ ㅜ ㅣ handwritten → read 아이 (ai, child)
- Series: "Part 2 tomorrow" → 21

**21. Hangul part 2: 5 consonants**
- ㄱ ㄴ ㄷ ㅁ ㅅ → read 나무 (namu, tree)

## C. K-culture moments (follow engine)

**22. What K-drama subtitles get wrong**
- Hook: "The subtitles lied to you. Here's what they actually said."
- Pick a common phrase, show literal vs subtitle

**23. Korean you need at Olive Young**
- 이거 있어요? (igeo isseoyo?, "do you have this?") / 얼마예요? (eolmayeyo?, "how much?")

**24. Korean you need in a taxi**
- 여기서 세워 주세요 (yeogiseo sewo juseyo, "stop here, please")

**25. Phrases for your first Korean in-laws dinner**
- Hook: "Three phrases that make a Korean mom love you."
- 잘 먹겠습니다 / 잘 먹었습니다 / 맛있어요

**26. Korean onomatopoeia**
- 두근두근 (heart pounding) / 쿨쿨 (sleeping) / 냠냠 (eating)

**27. Korean slang Gen Z uses**
- 갓생 (gatsaeng, "living your best productive life") / 킹받네 (kingbatne, "so annoying")

**28. A Korean reacts to how foreigners pronounce K-pop names**
- Hook: "You've been saying these names wrong. Kind of."
- Use group or member names only (no clips without permission)

**29. My students' most common mistake**
- Hook: "Everyone learning Korean makes this mistake in week one."
- 은/는 vs 이/가, keep it to one example

**30. Teach me a word from your language**
- Hook: "I taught you 29 Korean words. Your turn."
- Comment prompt; next video uses the top comment → builds community
